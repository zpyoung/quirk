# Airy White

## Character

A stark white canvas with large bold page titles, generous whitespace and neutral text. Rows are comfortable to spacious with hairline dividers, and the only saturated color is one soft blue call to action per view. Other color is confined to tiny icons and pastel tag pills. Radii are small to medium and depth is nearly absent, with a faint shadow on floating cards.

## When it fits

- Task lists, to-do and planning apps, and checklists with a secondary gray line under each title.
- Database-style pages with views, properties and pastel tags, such as trackers and wikis.
- Apps for people who open them briefly and often, where a clear page head and one obvious action help.

## Leaves out

- Notion's emoji covers and large emoji page icons, and its black cover art.
- Things' star glyph for Today and its set of per-list colored icons.
- Things' pink-red deadline color, so deadlines use the style's own warning token.
