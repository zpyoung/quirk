# Ink Panel

## Character

A retro comic look on warm paper. Controls and panels carry a 2px ink outline and a hard offset shadow down and to the right, so they read like raised keys: 3px on buttons, inputs, chips and toggles with 6px corners, 5px on cards, menus, popovers and dialogs with 8px corners. Rows inside a panel keep thin 1px dividers, and the sidebar keeps a quiet rule. Cobalt marks actions and the focus ring, which sits 3px outside the ink edge. Marker yellow fills the selected row, which also gets an inset ink edge and a 4px cobalt offset shadow. Dark mode flips the ink to cream on a night page, so the outlines and offsets keep the same shape. Type is Space Grotesk, with the system monospace stack for data cells.

Buttons and toggle chips lift 1px up and left on hover with a larger shadow, and press 3px down and right with the shadow gone. Panels, rows and menus never move. With reduced motion on, nothing moves and the shadow still changes. In high-contrast mode the borders stay and the selected row gets a system-colored outline.

## When it fits

- Personal and hobby trackers, such as a collection, a reading list or a habit log.
- Playful or creative tools where a bit of personality helps.
- Triage and issue queues, where one selected row has to stand out in a dense list.

## Studied from

- Sentry: the issue stream's filter group and table card sit on a solid, unblurred bottom edge, so buttons and panels read like raised keys, and the rounded bold type gives the page a comic-book feel. Ink Panel exaggerates that edge into an outline and an offset and keeps none of Sentry's colors or type. See `references/apps/sentry.md` and frames `references/media/sentry/S13-issue-stream.png`, `references/media/sentry/S13-resolve-menu.png`.

## Leaves out

- Sentry's aubergine base, its blurple, its severity pinks and purples, its font, logo, icons, illustrations and copy.
- Halftone dots, a display-lettering title face, speech-bubble shapes and any other comic ornament beyond outlines and offsets.
- Motion on anything but buttons and toggle chips.
