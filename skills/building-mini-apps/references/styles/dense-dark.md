# Dense Dark

## Character

Flat charcoal surfaces with the sidebar a step darker or lighter than the content, thin 1px dividers and almost no shadow. Rows are compact and text-first, selection is a solid color rather than a tint, and data cells use a monospace face at 12 to 13px. Layouts lean on three panes: a tree, a main list or grid, and a detail inspector.

## When it fits

- Data browsers, table editors and log or query viewers.
- File, note and knowledge-tree apps where the list of items is the main surface.
- Developer and operator tools used for long sessions where density matters more than breathing room.

## Studied from

- TablePlus: a charcoal data grid with a darker sidebar and inspector, a monospace grid with a muted line-number gutter, solid blue selected cells and field-type labels right-aligned in the inspector. See `references/apps/tableplus.md` and frames `references/media/tableplus/S3-the-interface-overview.png`, `references/media/tableplus/S3-filter-multiple.png`, `references/media/tableplus/S2-0080.png`.
- Obsidian: a near-black flat editor where the chrome is nearly the same tone as the content, a slim left ribbon and file tree of about 24px rows, a bold note title, and thin dividers. See `references/apps/obsidian.md` and frames `references/media/obsidian/S3-0012.png`, `references/media/obsidian/S4-bases.png`.

## Leaves out

- Obsidian's graph view and its violet link and active-tab color.
- TablePlus' green connection status pill and its syntax-highlight pinks.
