# Cool Tool

## Character

A light cool-grey utility interface built from 1px hairlines, small radii and compact rows in 11 to 13px sans. Group headers are full-width bands with a slightly darker tint, panels are white against a pale grey canvas, and rows carry little more than an ID, a status mark, a title and right-aligned chips. One muted blue-violet hue is used for selection and focus. Depth is flat apart from popovers.

## When it fits

- Issue, ticket and task lists that are grouped by status and scanned quickly.
- Inspector-style apps with a properties panel beside a list or canvas.
- Keyboard-first tools with filters, a command palette and dense metadata.

## Studied from

- Linear: tinted group bands with counts, rows of about 42px at capture scale with a fixed ID and status column, a properties sidebar with icon-plus-value rows, and a dimmed sidebar so content leads. See `references/apps/linear.md` and frames `references/media/linear/S1-t0066.png`, `references/media/linear/S1-t0111.png`, `references/media/linear/S2-t0114.png`.
- Figma: a four-region editor with white panels split by hairlines on a pale grey canvas, small muted labels over a tight input grid, and a floating bottom toolbar. See `references/apps/figma.md` and frames `references/media/figma/S1-1014.png`, `references/media/figma/S4-properties-panel.png`.

## Leaves out

- Linear's indigo selection color and its priority and status glyph set.
- Figma's azure blue and its multicolor brand, including the lavender and green marketing backdrops.
