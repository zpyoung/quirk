# Glass Launcher

## Character

A translucent, blurred panel with a large corner radius floating over whatever is behind it. It is near-achromatic: charcoal smoked glass in dark, frosted white in light, with a faint overlay for the selected row and a hairline border at low opacity. A large search input leads a single-column result list with generous row height and right-aligned type tags. Keycap chips and a bottom action bar carry the shortcuts, and one warm accent is used sparingly.

## When it fits

- Launchers, command palettes, quick-capture windows and switchers.
- Small utilities that open over other work, hold one list and close again.
- Mini-apps with a single search or pick step rather than a multi-pane workspace.

## Studied from

- Raycast: a smoked-glass panel of about 20px radius, a large search input, rows of about 36 to 40px with about 10px selected-row radius, right-aligned tags, keycap chips and a bottom action bar. See `references/apps/raycast.md` and frames `references/media/raycast/S1-0013.png`, `references/media/raycast/S2-0014.png`, `references/media/raycast/S4-action-panel.png`.

## Leaves out

- Raycast's red-lit gradient bleed behind the panel.
- Raycast's coral-red logo and the red keycaps and links used in its docs.
