# Calm Paper

## Character

A warm, slightly cream paper surface that is one flat plane, with almost no chrome around the content. Titles are a bold or regular serif over a neutral sans body; the effective accent is ink black, so primary actions are black filled buttons. Metadata sits in small outlined pill chips under the title, and floating elements (an input bar, a popover) are large-radius with very soft shadows. The reading column is narrow and rows are spacious.

## When it fits

- Note-taking, meeting notes, journals and other writing-first apps.
- Document collections and tables where the text is the content and tags are secondary.
- Apps used for long stretches by one person, where a quiet surface matters more than density.

## Studied from

- Granola: a warm off-white page with a serif title over sans body, a narrow column of about 600px, outlined meta chips for date and people, and a floating white pill input bar. See `references/apps/granola.md` and frames `references/media/granola/S1-0005.png`, `references/media/granola/S2-0017.png`, `references/media/granola/S5-enhanced-notes-2.png`.
- Craft: a near-white warm page held as a centered sheet, bold serif collection titles, pill toolbar buttons and tinted select-value pills in table columns. See `references/apps/craft.md` and frames `references/media/craft/S1-0017.png`, `references/media/craft/S4-introducing-collections.png`.

## Leaves out

- Craft's sky-blue gradient wallpaper behind the page.
- Granola's chartreuse green, which appears on chat bubbles, analyzing text and promo frames; the style keeps black as the accent instead.
- Both apps' wordmarks and marketing backdrops.
