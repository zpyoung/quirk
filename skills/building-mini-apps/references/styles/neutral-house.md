# Neutral House

## Character

Calm neutral greys with a faint cool cast, white cards on an off-white canvas, and one restrained slate-teal accent used for the primary action, the focus ring and selection. Corners are medium (0.5rem), borders are hairlines, and a single sans family (Geist) carries titles, body and data. This is the default every new mini-app gets unless a style is chosen.

## When it fits

- Any app where no style has been asked for; it makes no claim about the domain.
- Mixed-content tools that combine lists, forms and a table, where the interface should recede.
- A baseline to compare other styles against when deciding whether a change of look is worth making.

## Studied from

Not studied from an app. It is the house look the skill already shipped with, so there are no frames or study notes behind it.

## Leaves out

No brand traits are carried because none were borrowed. It also leaves out serif titles, translucency, a dark sidebar and monospace data cells, which belong to other styles.
