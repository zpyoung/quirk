# Split Tone

## Character

A dark charcoal sidebar set against a white content canvas, so the layering comes from the contrast between the two and not from shadows. Content sits on light grey hairlines with small radii of 3 to 6px, and items are pastel tinted blocks with a saturated stripe along the left edge. One saturated rose accent marks the active tab and the current-day marker; everything else stays neutral. Density is compact to comfortable.

## When it fits

- Calendars, schedules, timelines and agenda views.
- Apps that group items by category color, such as projects, calendars or labels, where each group needs a visible hue.
- Navigation-heavy apps with a persistent source list on the left and a light working area.

## Studied from

- Fantastical: a charcoal sidebar of event lines beside a white canvas, pastel event blocks with a saturated left-edge stripe, light grey hour-grid hairlines, a segmented view switcher, and a pale blue-grey detail inspector. See `references/apps/fantastical.md` and frames `references/media/fantastical/S1-0004.png`, `references/media/fantastical/S2-0012.png`, `references/media/fantastical/S4-calendar-views.png`.

## Leaves out

- Fantastical's red active segmented tab, which the style replaces with its own rose accent.
- Its weather glyphs in headers and the heat-map year view.
