## Unseen

- Keyboard navigation inside the Mini Window while it is open (arrows through the list, Return to open, Escape to close): described only as "easily navigated with just the keyboard" in S4, no footage.
- The expand action itself: no source shows the moment a Mini Window row or ⌘0 turns into the full window, how selection carries over, or whether the full window opens on the same date. Only the menu item and the ⌥-double-click rule (text, S5) are known.
- Detach and re-attach animation and the detached window's size and chrome: S1 shows the menu item, S4 describes dragging, no footage of it moving.
- Loading states (first sync, refresh after ⇧⌘R) and error states (offline, account failure, unparseable input): none appear in any source.
- Whether the popover keeps a half-typed entry when closed and reopened: not shown.
- What the parse card looks like for input the parser partly fails on: all typed examples resolved cleanly.
- Hover or focus styles for the toolbar and the mini-calendar arrows: only cursor positions are visible, with no visible change.
- Undo after adding or deleting an event: not shown.

## Patterns fed

- mini-full: a small menu-bar popover (input row, month grid, upcoming list, footer) and a full window whose sidebar repeats the same three blocks, opened by ⌘0 or ⌥-click, with the same quick-add card in both.
- list-peek: the dark upcoming list with date headers, dot + time range + title rows, dimmed past items, a pinned month grid above it, and the new row landing in time order after Return.
- assisted-notes: free-text input with a live parse card that updates field by field per keystroke, "/calendar" token picker, templates list, and highlighted parsed words with removable chips.
- view-switcher: the Day | Week | Month | Year segmented control (plus Quarter/Tasks via a ⌘1-⌘4/⌘R menu in the Mini Window) over one data set, with the sidebar unchanged between views.
- inspector: day view with a selected item opens a right column of fields, attachments and time zones, with selection color matching across grid, sidebar and inspector.
- workspace-panes: sidebar (22%) / content / optional right column (22%) that appears for search results or the inspector and narrows the center.
- expand-in-place: the compact quick-add card growing into a longer field list via "show more", and the matching chevron to collapse.
- action-palette: the gear menu and slash tokens as lightweight command surfaces, with shortcuts printed next to each item.
