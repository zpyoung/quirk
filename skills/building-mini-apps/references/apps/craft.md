## Unseen

- Keyboard-only use of a collection: arrow-key cell navigation, Enter to open a record, Escape to return, Tab between properties on the record page. The only on-screen shortcut shown anywhere was the page-insertion chord in S2-0012.png.
- A collection embedded in the same page as the opened record in a side panel or peek (no frame shows a peek; every open replaced the page).
- Loading and error states: no frame shows a spinner, skeleton, offline message or failed save. Only the empty state (S1-0019.png, S3-0025.png) and an empty record body (S1-0038.png) were seen.
- Drag-reorder of rows or of records between groups, and multi-row selection.
- Card or board view of a collection with the record opening from a card (the Kanban video rtHSVS8L3iY was not captured).
- What "Save" in the view toolbar (S4) does to the shared view versus the personal view, beyond the "1 change applied" label.
- Behavior on a narrow window: every frame is a desktop window of at least 1000 px.

## Patterns fed

- doc-collections → the whole pattern: a page is blocks; a collection block (title, tabs, toolbar, grid) sits among prose; each row is a full page with a properties list on top and a free body below; the breadcrumb and tab strip keep the record's parent chain; records also appear in the sidebar outline.
- view-switcher → named tabs under the collection title, each a saved filter/group/sort of the same rows; "+" adds a view and names it in a popover; unsaved changes are flagged ("1 change applied" with Save).
- inspector → right-edge panel with Insert/Format/Style/Info tabs, and the record page's property list as a label/value inspector with type-icon labels and inline popovers.
- expand-in-place → child pages collapse to a title plus one-line excerpt row, and open to a full page on click.
- list-peek → rows with a small open-affordance next to the title, and card tiles that show the first lines of the record.
- staged-table-edit → typed fields (title locked, select/multi-select/relation), edits in popovers with Cancel/Apply, filters built as chained popovers; view changes held until Save.
- action-palette → "/"-style insertion popover with search (typing "pag" lists Page) and a filter builder reached from a toolbar icon.
- workspace-panes → tab strip of open pages with a floating breadcrumb pill, sidebar that can be shown or hidden.
