## Patterns fed

- expand-in-place: a white, shadowed card replaces a one-line row, with title, Notes, and only the fields that have a value; empty fields collapse into a four-icon strip at bottom right, and each icon leaves the strip when its field is filled.
- inspector: the same field set (When, Deadline, tags, checklist, project label below the card) as a compact editing surface, with the footer bar swapping to move, trash, and more while it is open.
- list-peek: one-line rows with trailing glyphs for notes and checklist, two-line rows when the parent list is shown, right-aligned deadline text, "Show N more" links, and empty days as headings only.
- action-palette: small dark popovers anchored to a field or row (When, Deadline, Move, Quick Find) that accept typing, plus a right-click menu printing its shortcuts.
- view-switcher: fixed sidebar of date-based lists (Inbox, Today, Upcoming, Anytime, Someday, Logbook) above user lists, with counts and a Quick Find dropdown in the window title.
- mini-full: Quick Entry is the same card in a floating window with a destination footer, Cancel, and Save.
- assisted-notes: natural-language date entry ("in 3 days"), and notes that accept pasted links and bulleted lists that become a checklist.
- workspace-panes: an 18% sidebar plus a roughly 820px centered column; the footer toolbar changes with selection.
- doc-collections: Not fed by this app.
- staged-table-edit: only the multi-row selection band and the "moved out of the Inbox / OK" banner were seen; no staged editing.
