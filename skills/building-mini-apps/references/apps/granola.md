## Unseen

- Chat citations inside a chat answer: S4 says answers include citations, but no frame I could read shows what a citation marker looks like or where it opens.
- Typing a raw note while the transcript card is open on a wide window: S2-0016 shows it in a half-width window only.
- The moment a user line is edited inside an enhanced note: no source shows whether an edited AI line turns black.
- Selecting text in the note and then chatting about just that selection: S8 describes it in text, no frame shows the selection state.
- Keyboard focus order and arrow-key behaviour inside the template menu beyond the numbered rows and return hint in S5-enhanced-notes-3.
- Error states (offline, transcription failure, generation failure) and loading of the very first launch beyond `Analyzing transcript` and `Generating notes...`.
- Undo of a regenerate, and any diff between two generations.

## Patterns fed

- assisted-notes: the whole loop. Plain notepad with a bottom bar during capture, a floating transcript card, a generating banner with Cancel, black user lines vs grey AI lines with a hover magnifier that opens a source popover, and a bottom chat bar with recipe chips for follow-ups.
- action-palette: recipe chips above the chat input, the full Recipes grid, the numbered template menu with return hint, and Ctrl+K search.
- inspector: the magnifier popover (label, summary, quoted source) as a read-only provenance panel tied to one row.
- view-switcher: the two-segment toggle between the user's raw notes and the enhanced note, with a caret menu for templates.
- doc-collections: sidebar spaces and folders, dated groups (`Today`, `Yesterday`), and the empty folder state.
- list-peek: home rows with time at right and a hover-only menu and visibility label.
- workspace-panes: narrow docked notepad next to another app, collapsible sidebar with `Ctrl S`, floating transcript card and edge indicator.
- mini-full: the same note reflowing from a third-width pane to a full window, and the phone version with the same title, chips and status pill.
