- S4, text of the intro and the configuration list at https://www.home-assistant.io/dashboards/tile/: a tile "gives you a quick overview of an entity", has tap actions and features, and "the circular background behind an icon indicates that there is a tap action". `tap_action` defaults to the more-info dialog; `icon_tap_action` "by default will toggle the entity (if possible), otherwise, show the more-info dialog". So the two click targets are the icon (toggle) and the card (details), and the icon falls back to details for entities that cannot toggle. → feeds: command-center
- S4, text of the same page: features are extra controls placed at the bottom or inline (`features_position`, default bottom; with inline the first feature sits beside the name and the rest go two per row); a tile can be `vertical` (icon above name and state); `hide_state` removes the state; `state_content` can show `state`, `last_changed`, `last_updated` or any attribute, and `time_format` can make timestamps relative. A badge is shown on the icon for some entities (climate, person). The demo's "Downstairs" and "Upstairs" tiles are the page's own example of a bottom-aligned feature plus badge. → feeds: command-center
- S5, text of the intro at https://www.home-assistant.io/dashboards/badges/: badges "sit at the top of a dashboard view, above all the cards" and give "an at-a-glance overview of entity states" such as temperatures, open windows or who is home. The entity badge shows the state by default (`show_state` true, `show_icon` true, `show_name` false), and its tap action defaults to more-info. → feeds: command-center
- S5, text of "Entity filter badge" and "Conditions options": an entity-filter badge tracks a list of entities and shows only those that match its conditions (`state` equal or `state_not`, `numeric_state` with `above`/`below`, or `screen` with a media query), with examples such as lights left on or people who are home. Any badge can also be shown only to certain users or under a condition from its Visibility tab, and a shortcut badge runs an action (navigate, URL, assist, perform-action). A badge row can therefore be empty until something needs attention. → feeds: command-center
- S6, text of the intro and "Adding sections and cards" at https://www.home-assistant.io/dashboards/sections/: the sections view lets you organize cards in sections on a grid, with a maximum number of sections wide and an optional dense placement that fills gaps; creating a section adds a heading card at its top automatically, which can be deleted, and a section can be shown or hidden by conditions. A section can have a background color and opacity applied to the section itself, not to its cards. → feeds: command-center
- S6, text of "Editing the header" and "Header YAML configuration": the view header holds a title (Markdown and templating allowed) and badges, laid out `start`, `center` or `responsive` (the last is `start` on phones), with `badges_position` bottom or top; a footer card stays at the bottom of the view while scrolling and has a maximum width of 600 by default. → feeds: command-center
- S7, text of "Tap action" and "Options for confirmation" at https://www.home-assistant.io/dashboards/actions/: actions are `more-info`, `toggle`, `perform-action`, `navigate`, `url`, `assist` or `none`; the tap default is `toggle`, and "some cards overwrite default to more-info if the provided entity cannot be toggled". `confirmation` is off by default and can be a boolean or an object with `text`, `title` (default "Are you sure?"), `confirm_text` (default "OK") and `exemptions` (users for whom the dialog is skipped). Confirmation is something the dashboard author opts into per action. → feeds: command-center
- S8, the opening post by spacegaier (collaborator, 2021-04-12) at https://github.com/home-assistant/frontend/discussions/8905: actions on a row apply to its icon and label but not to the toggle or button the row renders, so a dashboard cannot guard an accidental click on that control. The post names the entities with a toggle (switch, light, fan, humidifier, automation) and those with a button (lock, cover, script) as the ones to guard, and proposes a confirmation with a text and per-user exemptions. → feeds: command-center
- S8, replies in the same thread: the maintainer bramkragten answers "Would just add a confirmation option then. A toggle should toggle, you should not change that behavior." The author later summarizes the agreement: a toggle leads either to the direct action or to a confirmation popup followed by that action or a return to the previous state, and tap, hold and double-tap on the toggle itself will not be configurable. Other users argue for a button that opens details instead of a guarded toggle, and ask for covers to be guarded against accidental opening; a 2024 comment says that the only guard at the time was "some faint warning/confirmation on the lock more-info". → feeds: command-center

## Unseen

- Keyboard use of the dashboard (tabbing across tiles, Enter or Space on the icon versus the body, Escape closing the dialog) was not shown in any source and was not tried in the demo.
- A stale entity in the tile itself. No source showed a tile whose last update is old; only the dialog's relative age ("12 minutes ago") and S4's `last_changed` option were seen.
- What an unavailable entity's details dialog looks like, and whether its controls are disabled there. S2 shows only the tile.
- A conditional or entity-filter badge appearing or disappearing, since the demo's three badges are always on. S5 describes the conditions in text only.
- A tile whose toggle carries a `confirmation` (S7), and the look of that confirmation dialog on a tile toggle. The demo's only confirmation is the inline "Really open?" button in a dialog; S8 describes the dashboard-side option and its mock-ups were not visible in the text.
- Cover controls and any cover confirmation: the demo's garage door dialog has no controls.
- Loading and error states of the dashboard beyond the demo's translation-404 banner on first load.
- Drag-and-drop editing of sections, the section background and theme options, and the footer card. S6 describes them in text and S3 only touches them in editor footage.
- Dark theme and the dashboard at tablet widths.

## Patterns fed

- command-center: tiles grouped under named section headings (a heading can show live values at its right), two click targets per tile (icon toggles, body opens details; the icon opens details for entities that should not flip), tile features inside the card (brightness slider, thermostat minus/plus, switch row) that act at once, a pill badge row above the sections with an entity-filter option that shows only entities in a given state, a details dialog with a fixed order (name and history icon, big state with relative age, main control, mode buttons) that becomes a bottom sheet on a phone, a sensor tile with a value and a sparkline and a details chart, unavailable tiles that keep their place but wear a grey icon, an orange "!" badge and an "Unavailable" subtitle, and confirmation as an opt-in per action with an inline second-press button ("Open door" then "Really open?") for the one risky action.
- action-palette: quick search from the toolbar, with category chips and domain labels on result rows, as the route to an entity that has no tile.
