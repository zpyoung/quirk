How we read the numbers

The times are medians from last quarter's warnings, measured from the warning being sent to the first acknowledgement or read receipt. Pager acknowledgements are faster partly because the pager asks for one.

When a different channel is better

If a warning has gone unread for 30 minutes during working hours, escalate it to the pager. If a service has no owner on shift, route its warnings to email, because chat only works when someone is watching it.
