Rules

A rule decides which alerts it matches, how they are grouped and where they go. Rules are checked from the top of the list and the first match wins.

Match

Matches on service, severity and labels. An empty field matches everything.

Order

Move a rule up to make it win over a broader one. Moving a rule is safer than editing it.

How the settings connect

Every setting on this page hangs off a rule or a channel. A rule names one channel and one escalation policy, and a channel names its owner and its members. Click a table to see its fields and what points at it.

Severity delays stand alone: they apply to every rule, so no rule points at them.

Window

How long the router waits after the first alert before it sends. A longer window sends fewer pages and delays the first one.

Delays

By severity

Each severity has its own delay. Setting a delay to 0 sends at once and skips the chance for an alert to resolve itself.

Quiet hours

During quiet hours a channel is muted, but critical pages still reach the pager.

quiet_hours:
  start: "22:00"
  end: "07:00"
  channels: [chat, email]

Channels

A channel is a place a person can be reached. Every channel has one owner and at least one member.

Members

Owners add and remove members. A channel with no members sends to its owner.

Types

  • Pager, for critical alerts.
  • Team chat, for warnings.
  • Email, for the daily digest.

Escalation

An unacknowledged page goes to the secondary on-call after 10 minutes and to the engineering manager after 15. Both times can be changed per rule.

Acknowledging

An acknowledged page stops escalating. It pages again if the alert is still open after an hour.

Audit log

Every change to a rule, channel or delay is recorded with who made it and when. The log is kept for 12 months and can be exported as a CSV from the settings page.
