Of the 42 alert settings the payments team uses, 9 are out of line with the team standard and 3 would page someone for a problem that cannot happen. Fixing those 3 removes about a third of last month's overnight pages.

Source: settings export of 12 Oct 2026, compared with the team standard of 1 Sep 2026.

Findings

  • 9 of 42 settings differ from the standard, and 6 of the 9 differ by being stricter.
  • 3 alerts watch a queue that was retired in August and still page at 03:00.
  • The digest is sent daily on 4 of 6 channels, where the standard is weekly.
  • No channel is without an owner.
