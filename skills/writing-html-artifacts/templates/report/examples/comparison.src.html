How the options were scored

We scored each option on the six rows above and weighed on-call load and setup time most heavily, because the platform team has two people and the order stream is on the critical path for checkout.

What would change the recommendation

If monthly volume passes eight times today's, Managed Pub/Sub costs more than $4,500 and self-hosted Kafka becomes the cheaper choice, though only if someone owns it. If the replay window has to grow past 30 days, the managed option needs a second store and the gap in cost closes.
