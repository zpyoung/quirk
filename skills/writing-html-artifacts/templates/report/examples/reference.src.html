What to do when the checkout service pages you: where to look first, how to roll back, and who to call.

Owner: platform team. Last reviewed 13 Oct 2026.

Acknowledging

Acknowledge within 10 minutes. An unacknowledged page escalates to the engineering manager at the 15 minute mark.

Muting

Mute an alert only during a planned change, and only for the length of the change. Write the reason in the channel when you do.

Rollback

If errors started within an hour of a deploy, roll back first and investigate afterwards.

Steps

  1. Open the deploy page and find the last version marked healthy.
  2. Choose Roll back to this version and confirm.
  3. Watch the error rate for 5 minutes. It should fall below 1%.

From the command line

checkout-deploy rollback --service checkout --to previous
checkout-deploy status --service checkout --watch

Latency

Check the region panel first. A single slow region points at that region's cache or network, and a slow everywhere points at the payment provider or the database.

Cache

The cache hit rate should be above 90%. Below 80%, flush the stale region from the cache console and watch the rate recover over 10 minutes.

Payment provider

The provider's status page is linked from the dashboard. If it reports a partial outage, move on to Failover.

Queue drain

The retry queue is safe to leave alone. Entries are persisted before they are attempted, so a restart loses nothing.

Workers stuck

If the oldest entry keeps growing while the depth is flat, restart the worker pool from the deploy page.

Backlog after an outage

Do not raise the worker count above 40. The provider's rate limit starts rejecting calls beyond that, which only lengthens the backlog.

Failover

The service can send payments through a second provider. Switch only when the first provider reports an outage or the health check has failed for 10 minutes.

checkout-config set payment.provider secondary
checkout-config show payment.provider

Switch back once the first provider has been healthy for 30 minutes, not before.

Billing data

When a charge fails, read these tables in order: the customer, the subscription that generated the charge, then the charge itself. Click a table to see every field and how it connects.

Promo codes

Checkout reads promo codes with a small expression parser. A code that is rejected with a parse error points at one of these classes.

Contacts

  • Platform on-call: page through the on-call tool, service name checkout.
  • Payments provider support: 24 hour line, in the shared vault under Providers.
  • Engineering manager: escalation at the 15 minute mark.
