Checkout p95 latency fell from 530 ms to 412 ms in week 41, and the cache change accounts for nearly all of it. One region, ap-southeast, still sits above the 500 ms target.

Source: edge timing logs for 5 to 11 Oct 2026, compared with 28 Sep to 4 Oct.

Findings

  • Median checkout latency is 186 ms, down from 241 ms in week 40.
  • The p95 is 412 ms, 22% lower than the 530 ms of the week before.
  • The cache change shipped on Tuesday 6 Oct and the drop starts the same day.
  • ap-southeast is the only region still above 500 ms at the p95, at 548 ms.

About these numbers

Latency is measured at the edge from request start to the last byte of the checkout response.

The p95 is the 95th percentile of all requests in the period, not an average of regional p95s.

Week 40 is the baseline each change is measured against.
